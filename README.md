# Jaap Harmsma

I build teams of AI agents that actually get real work done, running on my own hardware.

Website: [jaapharmsma.nl](https://jaapharmsma.nl)

## What I work on

- **Multi-agent systems:** a team of specialised agents (engineering, QA, security, research, content) coordinated by an orchestrator, with a shared knowledge layer.
- **Autonomy and safety:** deciding what agents may do on their own, which checks are tools and which are gates, and how to recover when something breaks.
- **Self-hosting:** a homelab running the whole stack, from reverse proxy to backups.

## Projects

- **[PolitiekCorrect](https://github.com/jgharmsma/politiek-correct):** a system of AI agents that gathers sources, runs the numbers on Dutch government policy and publishes the results with open methodology. The AI analyses, the reader decides. [Live site](https://politiekcorrect.com).
